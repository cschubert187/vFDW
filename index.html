What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a subfield of computer science concerned with building smart systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence. This includes learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, and natural language understanding.

Artificial Intelligence

The overarching umbrella. Covers everything from simple rules-based logical models (Expert Systems) to complex probabilistic architectures.

Machine Learning (ML)

A crucial subset of AI that allows software applications to become more accurate at predicting outcomes without being explicitly programmed.

Deep Learning & Neural Nets

A specialized branch of ML utilizing multi-layered artificial neural networks designed to mimic human cognitive capabilities.

Ethical Considerations in Academic AI

As artificial intelligence platforms become ubiquitously integrated into university ecosystems, ethical guardrails are necessary. Key concerns under study in Dallas College classrooms include:

  • Algorithmic Bias: Ensuring training sets do not reflect systemic inequities.
  • Academic Integrity: Navigating generative technologies as tools for collaboration rather than engines of original substitution.
  • Intellectual Freedom: Maintaining clear citations, open licenses (such as CC BY-NC), and attribution structures.

            // Replace

            // Wrap in standard doctype
